# 🍽️ SmartDine-Pro: Staff Operational Guide

Welcome to the new SmartDine-Pro ecosystem. This guide will help you navigate the new mobile app and kitchen monitor systems.

---

## 🤵 FOR SERVICE STAFF (WAITERS)

### 1. Shift Login
* **Login Credentials**: Open the SmartDine-Pro app. You must enter your **Registered Mobile Number** and your **4-digit Private PIN**.
* **Live Tracking**: As soon as you log in, the manager can see that you are **Live** on the main console. 
* **Shift Persistence**: You must keep the app active on your phone to receive real-time order alerts.

### 2. Handling Orders
* **Active Queue**: All orders for your assigned tables will appear in your "Service Dashboard."
* **Ready for Delivery**: When the kitchen finishes a dish, it will glow in the **"Ready"** section of your app.
* **Completion**: Once you serve the food to the guest, tap **"Mark Delivered"**. This is critical for accurate billing!

---

## 👨‍🍳 FOR KITCHEN STAFF (CHEFS)

### 1. New Order Alerts
* **Audio "Ding"**: The Kitchen Monitor now features an automatic audio alert. Whenever a customer places a new order, you will hear a **Notification Sound**.
* **Visual Queue**: New orders appear at the top of the list with a "Pending" status.

### 2. Production Workflow
* **Preparing**: Tap **"Start Preparing"** as soon as you begin cooking. This tells the waiter that the food is on its way.
* **Ready**: Tap **"Mark Ready"** the moment the dish is placed on the pick-up counter. The waiter's phone will vibrate/alert them immediately.

---

## 🏢 FOR MANAGEMENT (OWNER)

### 1. Staff Control
* **Adding Staff**: Go to **Billing Console ➔ Waiters & Staff**. Enter the staff name, mobile number, and set a 4-digit PIN.
* **Live Monitoring**: Look for the **Glowing Green Dot** next to staff names to see who is currently active on their shift.

### 2. Menu Pricing
* **Adding Items**: Use the **"+ Add New Dish"** button in the Menu & Pricing tab.
* **Categories**: You can create new categories (e.g., "Today's Specials") directly in the add-item window.

---

### 💡 PRO TIPS
* **Connectivity**: The system is optimized for **4G/5G mobile data**. If Wi-Fi is slow, switch to mobile data for faster syncing.
* **Security**: Never share your PIN with other staff members. Each delivery is tracked to your individual ID.
